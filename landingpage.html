What the Vendor Slide Deck Leaves Out

Six cost categories that rarely appear in vendor ROI models — but always appear on your invoice.

01
Agentic Loop Multiplier
A single task isn't one API call. Autonomous agents plan, observe, and reflect — creating context loops that multiply token spend by 4–10× per task.
4–10× token bloat
02
Non-Linear Concurrency Cost
Pay-per-token economics break down above ~50 concurrent users. Provisioned throughput adds $2,000–$6,000/month in fixed overhead before a single prompt is sent.
$2k–$6k/mo fixed
03
Data Pipeline Tax
Private AI and RAG deployments require continuous ETL compute and vector re-embedding as your data changes. Gartner: adds 15–30% to hosting cost.
+15–30% hosting cost
04
AI Cybersecurity Posture
Autonomous agents require prompt injection defense, behavioral monitoring, secrets management, and identity access controls absent from standard IT budgets.
$18k–$60k/yr
05
The Productivity J-Curve
BCG and McKinsey document a 10–20% output dip during the first 2–4 months of AI adoption. You pay for both the AI and the employee while productivity recovers.
10–20% dip, 2–4 months
06
Silent Scale Failures
AI fails at machine speed. Downstream data corruption from a single hallucination event cascades before detection. We model the rework cost of silent failures.
Cascade risk quantified
