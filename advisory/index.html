Frequently asked

What does the review actually deliver?

A 15–25 page document sized for a CFO or a board committee: fully-loaded employee cost per role, AI TCO across all seven cost dimensions, sensitivity to your top three assumptions, and a plain-English recommendation for each role. Plus the configured model — so anyone on your team can rerun it later.

Do you take vendor referral fees or equity?

No. The whole reason the review is worth doing is that it is not tied to a vendor. We do not accept referral, revenue-share or equity arrangements with any AI infrastructure or model provider.

How is this different from what a consultancy would produce?

A tier-1 consultancy delivers a slide deck built from interviews. We deliver a model, a document, and the configured spreadsheet. The number in the document is defensible because you can reproduce it, not because a partner signed off on it.

What if the review says "don't buy AI"?

Then that is the review. Two of the twelve roles in our public dataset come back that way at mid level (HR Generalist and Operations Analyst); more at junior levels. If yours land there, you have saved a budget cycle and a hiring reshuffle — which is worth a lot more than $5–15k.

Is the model open?

Yes. The methodology and code are on GitHub. You can audit every assumption before hiring us.
